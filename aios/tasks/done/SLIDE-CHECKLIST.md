## ⚠ Template fixes — do these before the other 34

Found by measuring Harry's nine exports. All three are **one fix in the template**, not nine fixes in nine files.

### 1 · Kicker — fixed ✅

Re-exported 24 Aug, 13:19. White where the ground is dark on 05, 07, 08 and 09; added to 01, which had none.

The numerals were also wrong in the first batch — every slide said "02". They now run 01 to 09. Worth a specific check on the other five cases, because it is the kind of error that survives a proofread.

### 1b · The SW mark — parked

Black-only, so it disappears on 02, 04, 05, 07, 08 and 09.

**Parked by Simon, 24 Aug.** The kicker carries the identification; the mark is a signature, not a label. Not worth blocking 34 slides over.

When it is picked up: the white files already exist at `2. Brand ID / sw-logo / sw-logo-white.svg` and `sw-logo-reversed.svg`, and the fix is one component with Light and Dark variants driven off the frame background. Settle at the same time whether the mark belongs on full-bleed photography at all — on 05 and 07 there is nowhere clean for it.

### 2 · Two frames are hugging their contents

Not fixed, and they moved in the second export, which is the tell:

| File | First export | Second export | Should be |
|---|---|---|---|
| `harry-05-imagery` | 2400 × 1351 | **2442 × 1397** | 2400 × 1350 |
| `harry-07-naming` | 2410 × 1361 | **2416 × 1371** | 2400 × 1350 |

A frame that changes size when you edit its contents is **hugging**, not fixed. Set both to a fixed W and H and turn **Clip content** on. Otherwise every edit shifts the crop and no two slides in the set are the same shape.

On 05 the frame had grown past its artwork and left a **24px black band along the top**. The build detects that step and trims it, then centre-crops — so the page looks right — but that is the build covering for the file, and it will not always guess correctly.

### 3 · Small type does not survive the page

The caption on 07 and the hex values on 04 are unreadable at mobile width, and close to it on desktop. The slide is shown at roughly 1200px wide on a laptop and 350px on a phone — that is a **7x reduction** from the 2400px artboard.

Rule of thumb: **nothing below 28px on the artboard.** Under that it is decoration, not information.

## Logged decisions

**Swearing stays.** (24 Aug) Harry's tone of voice slide carries *f-bomb* and *O-fuckin'-K*. Simon: *"Got to be loud and proud sometimes."* It is the client's authentic voice and it is real evidence of tone-of-voice work. **One typo to fix at source:** *"into conversation thats O-fuckin'-K"* — missing apostrophe on *that's*. Same sentence runs *"cheeky & tasty and if you feel like"*, an ampersand and an "and" three words apart. On the slide that sells the copywriting, both should go.
