## ⚠ The brand gate — built here, still open on the live site

Step 01 of the Website configurator now asks whether the client has a brand.

- *A Standard Work brand* — proceeds, no checklist, we already hold the files
- *An existing brand* — reveals a four-item checklist; brand prep is priced by
  what is missing and the total comes down as they tick
- *Not yet* — checkout is replaced by Brand 12,000 and Website 8,000 shown as a
  sequence, with *Start with the brand* as the primary action

Nothing hard-blocks: no error state, no disabled button, no dead end. The client
can always reach a human.

**On `standardwork.design` right now, none of that exists.** A person with no
logo, no typeface and no palette can pay 8,000 SEK today and arrive in the inbox
as a project that cannot be started — and will keep being able to until this is
merged.

That gap is the argument for merging, not a defect in the build.
