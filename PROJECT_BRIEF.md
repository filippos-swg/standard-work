# Project Brief — Standard Work™

---

## 1. What It Is

Standard Work is a fixed-scope design service for businesses that need clear, professional brand and website work without agency overhead.

It offers two defined packages with transparent pricing and a guided project intake process.

---

## 2. Core Positioning

**Build a brand and website for your business without agency processes and extra costs.**

The main value is not fixed price.

The main value is:

**Senior creative judgment made easier to buy.**

Fixed packages are the mechanism. Senior judgment is the value.

---

## 3. What It Is Not

- Not Fiverr
- Not an unlimited design subscription
- Not a cheap production service
- Not a generic design agency
- Not a bloated consultancy
- Not a SaaS template site

---

## 4. Services

### Brand for Your Business
Define and create the essential brand elements a business needs to look coherent and credible.

Includes: Logo, typography, colour palette, brand direction, imagery direction, tone of voice.

Base price: 12,000 SEK

### Website for Your Business
A simple, scrollable website that explains what the business does and makes it easy to get in touch.

Includes: One scrollable page, content sections, brand-aligned visuals, contact, desktop + mobile.

Base price: 8,000 SEK

### Add-ons (both packages)
Selected add-ons increase the base price. All pricing is shown live in the configurator.

---

## 5. Target Audience

- Founders and small business owners
- Freelancers and individual creators
- Businesses that have outgrown their current brand
- People who want professional results without an agency relationship

---

## 6. Business Model

- Fixed-scope packages
- Clear upfront pricing
- Payment split: 50% to start, 50% on delivery
- No ongoing retainer
- No open-ended processes

---

## 7. Constraints

- Scope must stay fixed. Add-ons are the only flexibility.
- Nothing sold that isn't defined. No exploration. No consulting.
- Voice must remain direct and plainspoken. No agency language.
- The site must feel like the service: structured, calm, confident, easy to buy from.
